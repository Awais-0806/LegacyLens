# Renovation Roadmap Engine

Phase 6 converts deterministic analysis, specialist findings, and health scoring into evidence-backed actions. It never edits repositories or executes code.

Actions retain source finding IDs and evidence references, controlled priority/effort/impact/risk values, prerequisites, acceptance criteria, and automation safety. Security-sensitive or uncertain work defaults to human review.

The roadmap uses stable action ordering, conservative blockers, quick wins only for low-risk small actions, and a staged sequence: baseline/safety, stabilization, documentation/observability, structural refactoring, incremental modernization, and validation/governance.

Missing evidence is represented as uncertainty rather than failure. Recommendations are not guarantees of runtime correctness and must be reviewed before execution.
